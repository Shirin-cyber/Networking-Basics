## 3.0 Introduction

### 3.0.1 Why should I take this module?

Kishori's back again, and this time we get a full day-in-the-life story. It makes a good setup for what this module is about.

She's just finished a **10-hour shift** at the hospital. Here's how the rest of her evening goes:

1. She's walking to her car when her phone rings. It's her son, **Shridhar**, reminding her to pick up the dinner he ordered.
2. She gets in the car and starts the engine. Her call **moves from her phone to the car speakers**.
3. She confirms she'll pick up the food and be home in an hour.
4. Before driving off, she does an **internet search** for the restaurant and taps the directions link.
5. The directions come through the speakers: *"Your destination is on the left."*
6. She buys the food and drives home.

At dinner, she tells Shridhar she's been thinking about all the technology she uses at home and at work. She knows **how to use it**, but not **how it works**. Her example is her phone. In one day it did calls, texts, an internet search, and driving directions. So how does one little device do all that? And how does it connect to all those different things?

Shridhar knows this stuff. He explains the types of networks mobile devices use:

- **4G/5G** mobile network
- **GPS**
- **Bluetooth**
- **NFC**
- **Wi-Fi**

And if I don't have a Shridhar to explain it to me, that's what this module is for.
