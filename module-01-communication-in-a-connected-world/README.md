## 1.0 Introduction

### 1.0.1 Why should I take this module?

So the course starts with Webster, who's basically our guide the whole way through. He introduces his friend **Kishori**, and her story actually makes the "why does this matter" part click.

Kishori has been a nurse at a hospital in Karnataka, India for about 20 years. When she went to nursing school, she had no idea she'd end up leaning on networking tech every single day. Here's her setup:

| | Devices |
|---|---|
| **At home** | laptop, smartphone, tablet |
| **At work** | laptop, desktop, printer, network-connected hospital equipment |

The problem is that these devices don't always talk to each other properly. At home that's just annoying. But in a hospital, if equipment stops working properly, **lives can be at risk**. That's a whole different level of stakes.

So Kishori wants to understand how it all actually works, and honestly that's the same reason I'm here. I use all these devices daily and have no idea what's going on behind the scenes. This module is where that starts.

- **Bandwidth and throughput** sounds like two words for the same thing, but they're listed together as speed *and* capacity, so I'm guessing they're related but not identical. I'll find out when I get there.
